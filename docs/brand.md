# FK RUG PULLS brand guide

## Story

An ordinary old man was rugged so many times that he stopped trusting promises and started documenting evidence. His journals filled with wallet addresses, timestamps, crossed-out claims, paranoid notes, and the same four words pressed through page after page: **FK RUG PULLS**.

The old man is part of the story, not a mascot. Do not turn him into a character logo, cartoon avatar, profile-picture collection, or recurring illustrated spokesperson.

## Voice

The voice is angry, deranged, obsessive, suspicious, and direct. It should feel like someone left a case file open under a bare bulb.

Use short commands and uneasy questions:

- FK RUG PULLS
- DON'T TRUST THE DEV. VERIFY THE DEV.
- RUGS SURVIVE IN THE DARK.
- CHECK THE FUCKING WALLETS.
- I TRUSTED THE DEV AGAIN.
- WHY DID THE TOP WALLET MOVE?
- MEMECOINS ARE FOR THE PEOPLE.

The anger points at secrecy, manipulation, and unverified claims. It must never become a promise of safety or profit.

## Colours

| Name | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ink black | `#0A0A0A` | Main backgrounds, type, borders |
| Paper white | `#F0EEE7` | Aged pages and main light surfaces |
| Hard white | `#FFFFFF` | High-contrast text on black or red |
| Warning red | `#D31313` | Warnings, strike marks, stamps, active emphasis |

Do not add crypto gradients, neon purple, Solana rainbow treatments, glossy gold, or soft startup pastels.

## Typography

- Headlines: extremely heavy, condensed-looking, uppercase sans serif.
- Evidence and data: monospaced type, like a typed case record.
- Journal notes: rough system handwriting used sparingly and at large sizes.
- Never make body copy look handwritten; evidence must remain easy to read.

The website uses system fonts so it has no external font dependency.

## Visual rules

- Use torn-paper tension, photocopy grain, ledger lines, stamps, tape, underlines, circles, and hard borders.
- Let a few elements rotate imperfectly, but keep addresses and body text straight.
- Use asymmetry and oversized type without breaking reading order.
- Keep contrast high and tap targets large.
- Never invent a mint address or place placeholder characters where an address might be mistaken for official.
- No mascot.
- No cartoon coin.
- No rockets, moons, candlestick hero art, glowing orbs, generic dashboards, or glossy 3D tokens.
- No visual claim that FRP eliminates risk or offers profit.

## Image treatment

Images, when needed, should look scanned, photocopied, taped, or pulled from a physical evidence file. Use black, paper white, and red. Avoid photorealistic people; the story should be suggested through the journals rather than represented by a stock-photo old man.
