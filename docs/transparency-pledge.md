# FK RUG PULLS transparency pledge

Status: **PRE-LAUNCH — NOT DEPLOYED**

There is no official FRP mint address yet. This pledge records the current locked ownership structure and how declared project-controlled wallets are intended to be used if deployment is later authorized. It does not guarantee profit, safety, liquidity, price stability, or continued value.

## Locked ownership structure

At the planned standard supply target of 1,000,000,000 FRP, the ownership structure is:

- **1% creator / creator trading wallet** — 10,000,000 FRP at a 1B supply.
- **1% operational Treasury** — 10,000,000 FRP at a 1B supply.
- **1% secondary Treasury reserve** — 10,000,000 FRP at a 1B supply.
- **97% public** — 970,000,000 FRP at a 1B supply.

The creator's personal beneficial ownership is capped at exactly **1% of total FRP supply**. Treasury holdings are separate project assets and do not count as creator personal holdings. Treasury assets must not be used to bypass the creator cap.

The Treasury represents 2% of total supply in total, split into two separately documented purposes: 1% operational and 1% secondary reserve.

## Plain-language rules

- Creator personal beneficial ownership must never exceed 1% of total FRP supply.
- Treasury holdings are separate project assets and do not count toward the creator's 1% personal cap.
- Treasury assets must not be used to bypass the creator cap.
- The intended total project-controlled structure is 3%: 1% creator and 2% Treasury.
- The remaining 97% is intended for public ownership.
- The creator must not use or control undeclared wallets, accounts, custodians, nominees, or other arrangements to secretly increase personal beneficial ownership above 1%.
- No presale.
- No private team allocation.
- The deployer wallet does not perform normal creator trading.
- Creator FRP activity is intended to occur through the published creator trading wallet.
- Treasury activity is intended to occur through the published Treasury structure and be documented publicly.
- The public should verify these rules on-chain instead of relying only on statements from the creator.

## What this pledge does not mean

Public wallets and published rules make activity easier to inspect. They do not remove market risk. Whales can sell. The creator can sell. Treasury assets can move for documented project purposes. The price can collapse. Transparency is evidence, not protection.
