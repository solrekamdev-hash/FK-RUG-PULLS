# FK RUG PULLS transparency pledge

Status: **PRE-LAUNCH — NOT DEPLOYED**

There is no official FRP mint address yet. Except for the finalized token allocation structure stated below, this pledge describes how project-controlled wallets are intended to be used if deployment is later authorized. It does not guarantee profit, safety, liquidity, price stability, or continued value.

| Allocation bucket | Finalized share or cap |

## The plain-language rules

- Exactly three project wallets are disclosed above.
- The project must not use or control any undeclared wallet, account, custodian, nominee, or other arrangement to buy, hold, receive, sell, transfer, or otherwise beneficially control a project allocation.
- No presale.
- Personal FRP activity is intended to occur through the published personal/trading wallet.
- The operational treasury is the primary project treasury.
- The deployer wallet also serves as the secondary treasury and is not a separate fourth wallet.
- Each project-controlled wallet is capped at 1% of total FRP supply.
- Total project-controlled allocation is 3%; public allocation is 97%; total allocation is 100%.
- The public should verify these rules on-chain instead of relying on statements from the creator.

## What this pledge does not mean

Public wallets make activity easier to inspect. They do not remove market risk. Whales can sell. The creator can sell. The price can collapse. Transparency is evidence, not protection.
