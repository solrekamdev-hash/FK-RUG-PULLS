02 / THE RULES

Promises are cheap / rules are visible

WRITE IT DOWN.
THEN WATCH IT.

This is the operating pledge for the project. It is not a promise of profit or safety. Four project wallets are disclosed, but only Personal, Operational Treasury, and Secondary Treasury are final project-controlled allocation buckets: 3% project-controlled and 97% public. Treasury locks are planned after launch, not completed.

  1. 01

    No undeclared FRP control

    The creator must not use or control any undeclared wallet, account, custodian, nominee, or other arrangement to buy, hold, receive, sell, transfer, or otherwise beneficially control FRP.

  2. 02

    No presale

    No private early purchase round and no hidden head start.

  3. 03

    Four wallets on the table

    The disclosed wallets are Personal / Trading, Dev / Deployer, Operational Treasury, and Secondary Treasury. Every address is public and distinct.

  4. 04

    Three final allocation buckets

    Personal is capped at 1%, Operational Treasury receives 1%, Secondary Treasury receives 1%, and Public receives 97%. Total final project-controlled allocation is 3%.

  5. 05

    Personal trading is visible

    Personal FRP buying and selling is intended to occur through the published Personal / Trading wallet, where it can be inspected. That wallet is capped at 1%.

  6. 06

    Deployer is transit only

    If deployment is authorized, the Dev / Deployer temporarily acquires 2%, transfers 1% to each treasury, and is intended to finish at 0%. Both treasuries have a planned 90-day lock after launch.
