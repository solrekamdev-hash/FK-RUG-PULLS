02 / THE RULES

Promises are cheap / rules are visible

WRITE IT DOWN.
THEN WATCH IT.

This is the operating pledge for the creator. It is not a promise of profit or safety. The 2% creator beneficial-ownership cap is finalized and permanently locked; the other launch settings remain planned.

  1. 01

    No undeclared FRP control

    The creator must not use or control any undeclared wallet, account, custodian, nominee, or other arrangement to buy, hold, receive, sell, transfer, or otherwise beneficially control FRP.

  2. 02

    No presale

    No private early purchase round and no hidden head start.

  3. 03

    No reserved allocation

    No reserved creator allocation and no private team allocation.

  4. 04

    Two percent maximum — locked

    Total creator beneficial ownership across all wallets, custodians, nominees, accounts, or other arrangements must never exceed 2% of total FRP supply. This finalized rule does not need to be re-decided before deployment, but compliance must be verified before and after launch.

  5. 05

    Trading is visible

    The creator can buy and sell FRP. This activity is intended to occur through the published creator trading wallet, where it can be inspected.

  6. 06

    Separate the deployer

    The deployer creates the token. It is not used for normal creator trading.
