I JUST NEED ONE FUCKING WIN.

001 / 005

Use the side controls or arrow keys to turn the page.

JUST LOOKING.

money is actually fucking unbelievable.

got paid friday and felt financially stable for roughly 14 minutes.

rent came out.

phone bill.

fuel.

food.

some subscription i apparently agreed to during a previous life.

then my car started making a ticking noise when i turn left.

not right.

only left.

so thats comforting.

checked my account this morning and just stared at it like

oh.

right.

we're doing poverty again.

i dont even wanna be rich rich.

like obviously if someone handed me 10 million im not gonna say nah mate keep it, i love checking my bank balance before buying chicken.

i just want one fucking win.

enough money that when something breaks i dont immediately start googling whether humans actually need both kidneys.

everyone else seems to have some secret little thing going too.

old mate at work has investments.

my cousin "does crypto" now.

some 22 year old on instagram is standing next to a lamborghini talking about discipline while wearing pants tight enough to affect circulation.

maybe im the dickhead.

maybe everyone else got sent the HOW TO NOT BE BROKE manual and mine got delivered to scott next door.

anyways the bloke at work mentioned that internet coin thing again.

said his mate made two grand off one.

two grand.

just like that.

i asked how and he started talking about market caps, liquidity, communities, all this shit.

i stopped listening around "two grand."

then he goes

"you just gotta know what you're looking at."

which sounds exactly like something said 30 seconds before a fucking disaster.

so obviously now i wanna know what im looking at.

not buying anything.

not doing anything stupid.

just looking.

literally just looking.
